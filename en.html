Designing the experience

I defined Stiddi’s concept, features and user journeys, then designed the interface to make personalised content easy to explore.

Building the product

I designed the prompts and personalisation logic, then coordinated the integration of the essential features: backend, authentication, user accounts and a Premium subscription.

Testing and preparing for launch

I am testing user journeys through TestFlight and on a device, investigating errors and checking fixes. I am also working on data security and preparing the app for an App Store launch.
